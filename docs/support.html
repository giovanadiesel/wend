Frequently asked questions

Does Wend record or send my videos anywhere?

No. All posture analysis happens locally on your iPhone. No video is recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

Does Wend replace physical therapy?

No. Wend is a complementary assistant, designed to support at-home exercise maintenance — it does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.

How do I report a problem or share a suggestion?

Send an email to giovanadiesel@outlook.com describing what happened — we're happy to help.
