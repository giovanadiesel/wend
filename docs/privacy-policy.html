Privacy Policy — Wend

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Wend was built on one core principle: your privacy comes first.

Local processing

Wend uses your iPhone's camera to analyze your posture during exercises. This analysis happens entirely on your device, using Apple technology (Vision and Foundation Models). No video, image, or movement data is recorded, stored on our servers, or shared with third parties.

Data stored

Information about your sessions (date, duration, accuracy) and your preferences (name, reminder times) is saved only on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share this data.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Wend schedules local notifications on your own device — this does not involve sending any data to external servers.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to giovanadiesel@outlook.com.
